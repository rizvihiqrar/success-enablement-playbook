# Release 4.1: Reminder Snooze

Released 2026-10-12.

## What Changed

A reminder can now be snoozed for 15 minutes, one hour or until tomorrow morning, straight from the reminder email or the in-app notice.

## Why It Matters

Customers said reminders that arrived at a bad moment were dismissed and then forgotten. Snooze keeps the reminder alive without opening the app.

## What To Tell Customers

- Choose **Snooze** in the reminder and pick a time. The reminder comes back at that time.
- "Tomorrow morning" uses the time zone set in the customer's profile. If reminders come at the wrong hour, check the time zone first (see the [reminders guide](../troubleshooting/reminders-not-arriving.md)).

## Known Limits

- A reminder can be snoozed up to five times.

## Guides Updated

- [Reminders not arriving](../troubleshooting/reminders-not-arriving.md): added the time zone check.
