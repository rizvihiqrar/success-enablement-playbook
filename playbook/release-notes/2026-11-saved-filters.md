# Release 4.2: Saved Filters

Released 2026-11-09.

## What Changed

Customers can now save a combination of filters (for example "assigned to me, due this week, high priority") under a name and open it again with one click. Saved filters appear in a new list on the left of the task page.

## Why It Matters

Teams with long task lists told us they rebuilt the same filters every morning. Saved filters remove that repeated work, and each person can keep their own set.

## What To Tell Customers

- Save a filter from the task page by choosing **Save this view** and typing a name.
- Saved filters are private to the person who creates them. Sharing with a team is planned and not available yet, so do not promise a date.
- A saved filter updates as tasks change. It stores the rules, not a copy of the tasks.

## Known Limits

- A person can save up to 50 filters.
- Filters that use a deleted team member show an "unknown person" label until the filter is edited.
