# Cannot Sign In

Use this when a customer cannot sign in, or says the password reset email did not arrive.

## Check In This Order

1. **Which address did they use?** After an email change, the old address no longer works. Ask which address they are typing.
2. **Reset the password.** Ask them to choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. Never ask for a password, and never reset one by hand for a customer.
3. **Reset email missing?** Ask them to check spam and wait five minutes before asking for a second email. Two emails in a row can make the first link stop working.
4. **Locked out?** After five wrong attempts the account is locked for 10 minutes. Tell them to wait, then try once with the reset link.
5. **Team account?** If the customer's company uses single sign-on, they must sign in through the company page, not with a password.

## If Nothing Fixes It

Collect the email address on the account and the exact message on the screen, and pass them to the support team. Do not ask for screenshots that show a password.

## What To Tell The Customer

Keep it short and calm: say what to try, in what order, and what you will do if it does not work. For example: "Please use Forgot password with the address you signed up with. If no email arrives in five minutes, tell me and I will pass it to our support team."
