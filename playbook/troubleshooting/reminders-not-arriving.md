# Reminders Not Arriving

Use this when a customer says a reminder did not reach them, or came at the wrong time.

## Check In This Order

1. **Is the reminder set?** Open the task and confirm a reminder time is shown. A task with a due date but no reminder sends nothing.
2. **Is the time zone right?** In the profile, check the time zone. A wrong time zone is the most common cause of reminders that arrive hours late or early. (Added after [release 4.1](../release-notes/2026-10-reminder-snooze.md), because "tomorrow morning" depends on it.)
3. **Is the channel on?** In notification settings, confirm that email or in-app notices are turned on for reminders.
4. **Is the email being filtered?** Ask the customer to check spam and to allow the sender address shown in the settings page.
5. **Was it snoozed?** A snoozed reminder comes back later. Look at the task history for a snooze entry.

## If Nothing Fixes It

- Ask for the task link, the time the reminder was expected, and the time zone shown in the profile.
- Pass those three details to the support team so they can check the delivery log. Do not guess a cause.

## What To Tell The Customer

Say what you checked and what is left. For example: "I checked your time zone and notification settings, and both look right. I have sent the details to our support team to look at the delivery log, and I will write again by the end of tomorrow."
