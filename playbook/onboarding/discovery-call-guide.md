# Discovery Call Guide

Use this on a first call with a new customer team. The goal is to learn how they work today, so the first week with Tasklane solves a real problem for them.

## Before The Call

- Read what the customer wrote when they signed up.
- Open a test workspace so you can show an example if they ask.
- Write down the three things you most want to learn.

## Questions To Ask

1. How does your team keep track of work today, and what is the most annoying part?
2. Who needs to see which tasks, and who is allowed to change them?
3. What happens when something is late? Who finds out, and how?
4. Which tools does your team already use that Tasklane should work alongside?
5. What would make you say after one month that this was worth it?

## What To Listen For

- A task that is "everyone's job" and so nobody's. Suggest an assignee for every task.
- Reminders that arrive on the wrong channel. Check the [reminders guide](../troubleshooting/reminders-not-arriving.md) with them early.
- Long lists nobody can sort. Mention [saved filters](../release-notes/2026-11-saved-filters.md).

## After The Call

- Send a short summary the same day: what you heard, what you will set up, and the next step with a date.
- Log any question you could not answer and tell the guide owner, so the gap gets fixed.
