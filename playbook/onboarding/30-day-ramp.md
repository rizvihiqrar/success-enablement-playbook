# 30-Day Ramp For New Customer Team Members

Who this is for: a new support or success team member for Tasklane, the imaginary team to-do app used in this playbook.

By day 30 you should be able to answer the ten most common questions without help, run a short product walkthrough, and write a clear note for the next person when you hand a case over.

## Week 1: Learn The Product

- [ ] Create a free test workspace and add three team members.
- [ ] Create tasks, set a reminder, and change who a task is assigned to.
- [ ] Read the [reminders troubleshooting guide](../troubleshooting/reminders-not-arriving.md) and the [sign-in guide](../troubleshooting/cannot-sign-in.md).
- [ ] Sit in on two live customer calls and write down every question you could not answer.

## Week 2: Learn The Customer

- [ ] Read the [discovery call guide](discovery-call-guide.md).
- [ ] Read the latest two release notes, starting with [saved filters](../release-notes/2026-11-saved-filters.md).
- [ ] Practise the three scenarios in the triage simulator until you can explain each answer.
- [ ] Answer five real questions with a teammate checking your replies.

## Week 3: Work With Support

- [ ] Take cases yourself with a teammate reviewing each reply before it is sent.
- [ ] Write one short article for a question you answered more than twice.
- [ ] Ask the owner of each guide you used whether anything was out of date.

## Week 4: Show What You Know

- [ ] Run a ten-minute walkthrough of one feature for the team.
- [ ] Hand over one case with notes that a stranger could follow.
- [ ] Meet your manager: what is clear, what is still confusing, and which guide helped least.

## How We Know It Worked

- You can answer the ten most common questions without looking them up.
- Your replies need fewer edits each week.
- You have named at least one guide that needed a fix, and it was fixed.
